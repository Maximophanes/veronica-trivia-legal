Veronica Trivia — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2026

Veronica Trivia is a free, unofficial fan-made knowledge game hosted on Reddit and maintained by u/Maximophanes. This policy explains what information the game uses, why it is needed and how you can manage or delete it.

Information we collect

Veronica Trivia uses identifiers provided by Reddit to associate your gameplay with your account or guest session.

If Reddit does not provide a guest identifier, Veronica Trivia generates a temporary guest credential when you start an assessment. Your browser may retain this credential in local storage so you can access and delete your saved guest game data. The game stores a hashed version of the credential rather than the credential itself. It expires within seven days of creation or the start of your most recent guest assessment.

The game stores:

  • Your Reddit-provided player identifier or an identifier derived from the temporary guest credential.
  • Interactive post and assessment identifiers.
  • Question and answer order, submitted answers and assessment status.
  • The date and time each question was last shown to you, if you are signed in.

Guest players do not have question encounter histories.

How we use your information

This information is used to operate the game, maintain assessment progress, grade answers consistently, prevent duplicate submissions and prioritize questions you have not previously encountered.

Veronica Trivia does not use your information for advertising, profiling, artificial intelligence training or unrelated purposes.

Data storage and retention

Game data is stored within Reddit's developer platform.

Your browser may remember whether you turned off Atmospheric effects and which version of Update Notes you last opened. These preferences stay in your browser and are separate from game data stored on Reddit. You can clear them through your browser’s site data controls. Clearing site data may also remove a temporary guest credential. If that happens, Veronica Trivia may no longer be able to identify your previous guest assessments for self-service deletion. Those assessments keep their original expiry deadlines.

  • Assessment data: Automatically expires seven days after the assessment begins.
  • Question encounter history: Automatically expires 30 days after each individual question was last shown to you.

Veronica Trivia does not maintain an external game database, analytics service or public leaderboard.

Data sharing

Veronica Trivia does not sell player data or share it with advertisers or external analytics services.

Reddit hosts the game and processes information in accordance with its own Privacy Policy.

Deleting your information

You can delete your saved game data at any time through:

Assessment setup → Privacy & terms → Delete saved game data

This removes your saved assessments and question encounter history from the current game installation.

If you play using a temporary guest credential, you can delete your saved guest game data while your browser still holds that credential. If the credential is lost, such as when browser storage is cleared, the game can no longer identify your previous guest assessments for self-service deletion. Those assessments automatically expire within seven days of their creation.

The game also periodically checks account availability and removes saved game data for accounts Reddit confirms are no longer available.

If you experience difficulty deleting your data, or wish to make a privacy-related request, contact the creator. Applicable deletion requests will be handled within ten days, or sooner where required by law.

Your privacy rights

You may contact the creator to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, or to exercise other applicable privacy rights.

Website privacy

This policy and the Terms of Service are hosted on GitHub Pages. These pages do not use additional analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or cookies set by Veronica Trivia.

GitHub may process visitor information for hosting, security and operational purposes in accordance with its own Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Veronica Trivia develops. The current version will remain available on this website, with its effective date displayed above.

Contact

For privacy questions, deletion requests or assistance, contact u/Maximophanes on Reddit.
